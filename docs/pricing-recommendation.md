# Pricing recommendation — pilot proposal

Status: Research-backed launch hypothesis; validate with pilot customers before publishing

## Product principles

- Charge per **business**, not primarily per seat: this matches the small-business customer and the
  multi-business access model.
- Keep mandatory country compliance in the relevant subscription tier. Never use credits to block a
  legally required invoice, audit record, or correction.
- Use credits only for optional, metered value with a real marginal cost, such as AI assistance,
  document scanning, SMS/WhatsApp delivery, storage overages, or premium integrations.
- Offer annual billing at a discount, but do not require a long contract for the launch customer.
- Do not publish custom-development prices as a feature menu. Sell a scoped outcome with a written
  change order, delivery acceptance, support boundary, and upgrade responsibility.

## Proposed subscriptions

Prices are per business per month before applicable tax. Annual prices are approximately 20% lower.
Each paid plan includes ordinary email support and the country pack required by that business.

The proposal deliberately places India below Saudi Arabia and the UAE while retaining a comparable
feature ladder. A business moves by operational need—users, documents, workflows, locations,
compliance workload, or support—not merely by revenue.

## Credits

Do not launch credits with the minimum operational release. Introduce them only after the underlying
usage is reliable and visible to the customer. Every credit event needs a clear unit, current
balance, pre-use estimate, immutable usage record, refund policy, and low-balance warning.

Candidate future uses: AI actions, receipt scans, SMS/WhatsApp messages, unusually large exports,
and premium integration calls. Do not meter ordinary logins, customers, invoices needed for legal
compliance, or access to historical records.

## System-administrator complimentary access

The bizOS system-administrator team may grant a selected business complimentary access to a paid
plan, module, service, or credit allocation. It may also create a negotiated commercial agreement: a
percentage or fixed discount, a fixed local-currency contract price, included services, a tailored
package, or a credit grant. This is an entitlement and price override, not a bypass of the
compliance, authorization, or audit model.

Every complimentary or negotiated agreement records the business, granting administrator, scope,
reason, start date, expiry or review date, normal list price, agreed price or discount, currency,
and approval evidence. The business sees its applicable terms and review date. An agreement expires
or reverts to the published plan automatically unless renewed by an authorized administrator.

## Pilot validation gates

Before publishing, interview at least ten prospective customers in each launch market and test the
pricing page with three segments: freelancer/micro business, small service business, and small
construction business. Validate willingness to pay, paid conversion, setup completion, cost to
serve, support time, country compliance cost, and churn risk. Review prices quarterly and when
regulator or provider costs change.

## Research anchors

Zoho Books is a comparator, not a product specification. Its published Saudi plans include free, SAR
69 monthly Standard, and SAR 129 monthly Professional tiers; its Saudi free plan is limited by
annual revenue and document volume. Its published India plan begins at INR 899 monthly or INR 749
when billed annually, while a UAE reseller listing begins at AED 69 monthly. These anchors support a
country-local entry price rather than a single converted global price.
